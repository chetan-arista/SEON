
Top Article Platforms by Goal

• For Building a Brand or Business (WordPress.org):
	• Best for: Complete ownership, scaling, custom design, and full monetization (ads, products, affiliate links) without platform cuts.
	• Cost: Free open-source software, plus domain (~$15/year) and hosting (from ~$3/month).
• For Direct Subscriber Newsletters (Substack):
	• Best for: Writers building a direct email list and monetizing via paid subscriptions.
	• Cost: Free to start; takes 10% of paid subscriptions.
• For Built-In Readers & Essays (Medium):
	• Best for: Creative nonfiction, essays, and tapping into an existing reader base right away without managing a website.
	• Cost: Free to publish; earnings depend on the Partner Program.
• For Professional & B2B Visibility (LinkedIn Articles):
	• Best for: Career updates, business insights, industry thought leadership, and landing high-paying clients.
	• Cost: Free.
• For Technical & Coding Writing (Hashnode / Dev.to):
	• Best for: Software engineers, tutorials, and developer communities.
	• Cost: Free
